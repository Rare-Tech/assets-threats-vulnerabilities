# 📊 Risk Assessment Report

## 🔍 Summary

An assessment was conducted to identify organizational assets, threats, and vulnerabilities affecting SecureCorp’s environment.

---

## Key Findings

* Sensitive data assets face elevated risk exposure
* Weak authentication increases unauthorized access risk
* Lack of monitoring reduces threat visibility
* Employees may be vulnerable to phishing attacks

---

## Risk Evaluation

| Threat              | Likelihood | Impact | Risk Level |
| ------------------- | ---------- | ------ | ---------- |
| Phishing            | High       | High   | High       |
| Malware             | Medium     | High   | High       |
| Unauthorized Access | High       | High   | High       |
| Insider Threat      | Medium     | Medium | Medium     |

---

## Security Recommendations

* Implement Multi-Factor Authentication
* Conduct employee security awareness training
* Regularly patch and update systems
* Apply least privilege access controls
* Improve security monitoring practices

---

## Conclusion

This assessment highlights the importance of proactive security controls, employee awareness, and continuous monitoring in reducing organizational cybersecurity risks.
